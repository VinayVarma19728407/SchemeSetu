# SchemeSetu - User Manual

Welcome to **SchemeSetu**, the centralized Central Government Scheme Discovery Platform. This manual will guide you through the features available on the platform and how to use them to find government schemes tailored for you.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Registration
1. Click on the **Register** button in the top right corner of the navigation bar.
2. Enter your Full Name, Email Address, and a secure Password.
3. Click **Sign Up**. Your account will be created, and you will be automatically logged in.

### 1.2 Login
1. If you already have an account, click **Login**.
2. Enter your registered Email Address and Password.
3. Click **Login** to access your Dashboard.

---

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard

Once logged in, you will be taken to your Dashboard. Here you can see:
- **Your Bookmarks**: A quick count of the schemes you have saved.
- **Profile Completion**: A percentage showing how much of your demographic profile is complete.
- **Bookmarked Schemes**: Quick access to your recently bookmarked schemes.
- **Quick Actions**: Shortcuts to Browse Schemes, Find Schemes for Me, and My Profile.

---

## 3. Completing Your Profile

To get accurate scheme recommendations, you should complete your demographic profile.
1. Navigate to **My Profile** from the Dashboard or the top navigation bar.
2. Under **Demographic Profile**, fill in your details such as Age, Gender, Occupation, Annual Income, State, and Social Category.
3. Click **Save Profile**. Your completion percentage will update accordingly.

---

## 4. Discovering Schemes

### 4.1 Browse Schemes
If you prefer to manually search for schemes:
1. Click **Browse Schemes** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the search bar to find schemes by keywords.
3. Use the filters on the left side to narrow down schemes by Ministry, Category, or Target Audience.

### 4.2 Find Schemes for Me (Eligibility Match)
To let the system find schemes for you:
1. Click **Find Schemes for Me** in the navigation bar.
2. Ensure your profile is up to date. The system will use your profile data (like Income, Age, and State) to instantly match you with schemes you are eligible for.
3. The platform may prompt you with specific eligibility questions depending on the schemes available.

---

## 5. Scheme Details & Bookmarking

### 5.1 Viewing Details
Click on any scheme card to view its detailed page. You will find:
- Scheme Introduction and Overview
- Detailed Benefits
- Full Eligibility Criteria
- Required Documents
- Application Process Steps

### 5.2 Bookmarking
- To save a scheme for later, click the **Bookmark icon** (ribbon) on the scheme card or on the top right of the scheme details page.
- You can access all your saved schemes by clicking **Bookmarks** in the top navigation bar.
